# Demo guide — what to show, in order

## Opening (10 seconds of magic)
Open the production build. Do nothing. The globe is already alive: day/night terminator in the right place for this exact minute, night-side city lights, ~2,500 real aircraft as a golden shimmer, 460+ camera pins worldwide.

## The Surprise loop (2 minutes)
1. Click **Surprise me**. Weighted toward spectacle: ISS orbital pass with live TLE-propagated path, volcanoes venting on live cameras, NASA's full-disk Earth photo from a million miles away.
2. On a camera card: note the honest copy — night-aware ("it is night at the camera site"), daylight-aware aurora cameras.
3. Click **Surprise me** twice more — no immediate repeats, no adjacent-region repeats.

## The investigation rail (3 minutes — the strongest differentiator)
Click **Investigate**. Top items right now:
- **The attention gap** — Ukraine vs Sudan coverage per million displaced, same-hour measurement.
- **The empty-sky audit** — live ADS-B: Ukraine/Sudan/Syria/Yemen silent vs 250-340 aircraft in control skies. The bar chart IS the story.
- **The planet's emergency board** — all ~156 GDACS active disaster models, most never in your feed.
- **The air divide** — same-hour PM2.5 across 16 megacities; the ratio recomputes itself hourly.
- **24 volcanoes actively erupting** — Smithsonian consensus feed, cross-linked to live volcano cameras.

## The connection web (1 minute)
From the volcano investigation: tap a **LIVE LOOK-INS** chip — jumps straight to Etna/Kīlauea/Iceland live on the globe. The camera card shows "Part of the investigation" to jump back. Works both directions.

## Globe physics (1 minute)
Drag the globe. Tap any camera pin — live look-in with ranked nearby alternates. Tap empty ocean/land — place probe with nearest cameras, local seismic/weather context.

## Share (30 seconds)
On any card, tap Share — copies a `?d=` link. Paste it in Slack/iMessage: the preview resolves the actual live title and image server-side.

## Watch mode (ambient)
Tap Watch — cinematic auto-tour, hero copy fades, planet commands the screen. This is the lobby-screen mode.

## If asked about data honesty
Every card labels evidence as observed/verified/interpretation/question. Freshness is honest ("positions ~40s old", model vs measurement). Nothing is fabricated; absence of data says so.

## Round VIII–X additions (newest first)
- **My sky** — dock button. Grant location once; the probe reads aircraft within 100 nautical miles *overhead*, nearest live cameras, weather, hazards. Coordinates rounded to ~1 km on-device before leaving the browser. (This is the personal hook — try it live on the call.)
- **The living planet layers** — the globe itself is now the data: earthquake belt glowing along the Ring of Fire (brighter = fresher, a full week deep), active fires, storms, volcanoes, and NOAA's aurora oval as a green polar ring that expands with geomagnetic activity. Legend bottom-left.
- **Breaking toast** — if a major quake/fire enters the feed while you're watching, it announces itself top-center; tap to travel there.
- **Memory** — return visits show "N new live moments since…" (per-device).
- **Sound** — generative ambient drone tuned per discovery type (WebAudio, zero assets). Toggle in the dock.
- **New cards**: wildlife pulse (iNaturalist live sightings with real observer photos), Earth's extremes (hottest/coldest/windiest at the same minute across 16 extreme sites), humanity's day/night split (population-weighted: "248 million in night right now"), Point Nemo moment (when ISS astronauts become the nearest humans to the loneliest place on Earth — conditional, only appears when true).
- **New investigations**: the sunset chase (every airborne aircraft classified by solar elevation — "126 aircraft are flying through Earth's sunset line"), wildest water (live wave heights across 14 seas — "Drake Passage running 15× rougher than the Red Sea").
- **Camera rotation** — the Surprise loop now rotates quality gems from the full 451-camera registry every 30 minutes instead of the same 14 cameras; taps on related pills ("Wildest water", "Terminator") open the real investigation/card, not generic weather.

## Round XI additions
- Satellite time-lapse: any GOES imagery card has a "▶ time-lapse" toggle — hours of cloud motion in one loop. Best demo: any NWS alert or fire card in the US.
- My sky: dock button probes the sky above the viewer (aircraft within 100nm, nearest cameras, weather) — coords rounded to ~1km on-device before leaving the browser.
- Notable-nearby: globe taps now surface real Wikipedia landmarks (museums, parks, monuments) with distances — administrative noise filtered.
